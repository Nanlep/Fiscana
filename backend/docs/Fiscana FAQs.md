

# 📚 Fiscana FAQ  
**What is Fiscana?**

Fiscana is a digital financial management and SME financing platform that helps businesses:

* Track income & expenses  
* Generate financial statements  
* Build credit profiles  
* Access financing

---

### **Who can use Fiscana?**

* SMEs  
* Startups  
* Schools  
* Traders & service providers  
* MSME clusters & cooperatives

---

### **What can I do on Fiscana?**

* Create invoices & receipts  
* Track income & expenses  
* Manage assets & liabilities  
* Generate:  
  * Profit & Loss  
  * Cashflow statements  
* Get AI-based financial insights  
* Apply for SME financing

---

### **What is Smart Ledger?**

Smart Ledger automatically organizes your:

* Income  
* Expenses  
* Transactions

Into structured financial records lenders can trust.
